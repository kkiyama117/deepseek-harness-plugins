### Rules

1. Definitions, as now: `lineage = implementer_lineage(REVIEW_ROUTE)`; `rounds` = the history's `round` records in `seq` order; `latest_round` = the last of them or `None`; a fixed high is a finding whose `FINDING` match has severity `high` (any case) and whose findings.tsv row has disposition `fixed`.
2. `round2_case` (the round-2 case) is true when all of these hold:
   - `rounds` has a record with `round` 1;
   - no record in `rounds` has `round` 2;
   - some history `fixcheck` record whose `seq` is greater than the `seq` of the first `round` 1 record has a fixed high, and its `state` differs from the current `state`.
   Only `fixcheck` records are candidates. A `round` record, the round-1 record included, is never a candidate: a fixed high found in round 1 leads to a fix check, and a lead who wants a full second review passes `--kind round --reason ...`.
3. `derived` is decided in this order, and the first that holds wins:
   1. `'round'` when `round2_case` is true (the round-2 case);
   2. `'round'` (a further reviewer of the same round) when `latest_round` is not `None`, `latest_round['state'] == state`, and no record in `rounds` has `lineage == lineage` and `round == latest_round['round']`;
   3. `'round'` (the first round) when `rounds` is empty;
   4. `'fixcheck'` otherwise.
4. Round numbering when the chosen kind is `round`: when `derived` was decided by rule 3.1 (the round-2 case), the round is 1 + the highest `round` in `rounds`, whatever the state. Otherwise it is as now: `latest_round['round']` when `latest_round` exists and its `state` equals `state`, else 1 + the highest `round` in `rounds` (1 when there is none). This applies to a `--kind round` override too.
5. Every other part of `_review` stays as it is: the dispose check, the `--kind`/`--reason` handling, its printed lines, `covers`, `covers_from`, and the fixcheck brief.
