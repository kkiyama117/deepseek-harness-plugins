## T24. orchestrate-agents: count only spaces as indentation; refuse a log cut inside an inherited marker

Issue: #45.

Needs: T23 (committed: the config block bound in `session-log-path.mjs` and the seeded-marker check in `session-route.mjs`).

Spec:
- Second review of T23 (gpt-6-astra, privacy): two real findings this card fixes.
  1. `session-log-path.mjs` measures indentation with `trimStart()`, which also strips non-ASCII whitespace such as U+00A0. A crafted dump whose later key is indented with two spaces and two U+00A0 (`    meta:`) does not end the config block, so `    compression: SECRET-VALUE-123` under it is read and printed in the error. `tasks/T20.md` and `tasks/T23.md` say "leading spaces", meaning U+0020 only.
  2. `session-route.mjs` takes the last tagged `session/end-seed` marker as the child's. In a multi-level fork, the copied prefix already holds an ancestor's marker; when the child's own marker is the incomplete final line that Reading step 4 drops, the ancestor's marker becomes the last one, and the parent's events are printed as the child's.
- `tasks/T21.md` (Reading steps 4 and 6) and `tasks/T23.md` (the seeded-marker check): the rules this card extends; every other rule stays. The lead updates `references/run-dsh.md` in a separate commit; it is not part of this card.

### Rules

`session-log-path.mjs`:

- Every count of leading spaces in the script counts only U+0020 characters from the start of the line (the count stops at the first character that is not U+0020). This applies to the config block bound, the `>-` fold continuation rules, and every other place that measures indentation.
- A line is empty when it is empty or holds only U+0020 characters. A line that holds other whitespace is not empty.
- Nothing else changes.

`session-route.mjs`:

- In Reading step 4, when the text does not end with `\n`, keep the dropped last piece (it may be a whole record without its newline; the backend discards such a line too). After Reading step 6 and the T23 seeded-marker check, and before Reading step 7, refuse the log when the dropped piece is non-empty and either:
  - it contains the substring `session/end-seed`; or
  - the first parsed event is a `session` header with `isSeeded === true`, and the string `{"type":"session/end-seed"` starts with the dropped piece (the cut fell inside the marker's opening).

  To refuse: print nothing to stdout, print `session-route: <LOG>: the log ends in an unfinished inherited marker` to stderr, and exit 1.
- Nothing else changes; any other dropped last piece is still ignored.
